DepthAnythingV2SmallF32P8.mlpackage

Depth Anything V2 Small, converted to Core ML by Apple and taken from
https://huggingface.co/apple/coreml-depth-anything-v2-small (the F32P8
variant, 8-bit palettized weights).

Original work: Depth Anything V2, Lihe Yang et al., 2024.

Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0; a copy is beside this file in
DepthAnythingV2SmallF32P8.LICENSE.txt.

Instant uses it to estimate a photo's depth on the phone for the 3D button.
See wiki/ios-client.md and wiki/decisions.md.
